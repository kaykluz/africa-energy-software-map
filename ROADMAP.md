# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation

- Product charter and scope
- Information architecture and interaction contract
- Data model, taxonomy, and stable IDs
- Evidence and verification policy
- Source register and editorial workflow
- Export and API contract
- Autonomous-agent safety and review design

## Phase 1 — Software map

- Stack, Deployments, and Directory views
- Product, organisation, and light country pages
- Moderated submission, correction, and profile-claim flows
- Private evidence-review and operations workspace
- Scheduled retention and proposal-only research preparation
- Versioned public data exports
- Initial curated launch census

## Phase 1.1 — Research and reuse

- “What the map shows” insights
- Sector navigation across the same reviewed product set
- Factual comparison and shortlist
- Public read API
- Multilingual groundwork
- Automated source monitoring and candidate pull requests

## Phase 2 — Organisations and service providers

- Developers, owners, EPCs, O&M providers, OEMs, advisers, utilities, regulators,
  programmes, and associations

## Phase 3 — Projects and assets

- Public project records and evidenced project-party relationships

## Phase 4 — Capital and transactions

- Funds, instruments, lenders, investors, guarantees, transactions, and mandates

## Phase 5 — Markets and live intelligence

- Country market structures, regulation, infrastructure links, opportunities,
  tenders, and alerts
