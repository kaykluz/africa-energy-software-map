# Corroborate and challenge

Version: 1.0.0

Review a proposed assertion adversarially.

Ask:

- Does the source actually name the product, customer/project, and geography?
- Is the source independent of the provider?
- Is it original reporting or syndicated provider copy?
- Does it establish a deployment, or only availability or partnership?
- Is the date a deployment date, announcement date, or article date?
- Does a named customer confirm the relationship?
- Is there conflicting or more recent evidence?
- Could the information expose sensitive infrastructure?
- Is the candidate a duplicate, rename, acquisition, or historical record?

Recommend `editorial_review`, `request_evidence`, `possible_duplicate`, or
`reject`. Do not upgrade evidence status yourself.
