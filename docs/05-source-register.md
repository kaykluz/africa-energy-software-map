# Source register

The source register is a maintained inventory of places the research process may
monitor. It is not evidence by itself.

## Source families

- National regulators, ministries, utilities, system operators, and power pools
- Public procurement and award portals
- DFI, donor, challenge, grant, and implementation-programme databases
- Provider product pages, case studies, changelogs, and repositories
- Customer announcements, annual reports, and project pages
- Financing, transaction, and project-close documentation
- Research institutions, academic publications, and conference proceedings
- Sector associations, accelerators, and ecosystem programmes
- Reputable trade and business reporting

## Register fields

- ID and name
- Source family and countries
- Relevant categories
- Base URL and discovery method
- Access and automation constraints
- Expected update frequency
- Default independence class
- Language
- Last checked and next review
- Active, paused, or retired status
- Notes and known limitations

The working template is
[`data/templates/source-register.csv`](../data/templates/source-register.csv).

## Monitoring

Automated monitoring may detect new or changed material, but every candidate
enters the editorial queue. Robots directives, access terms, rate limits,
copyright, privacy, and source-specific restrictions must be respected.
