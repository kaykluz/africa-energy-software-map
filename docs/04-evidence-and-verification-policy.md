# Evidence and verification policy

## Principle

AI, contributors, providers, and aggregators may identify claims. Evidence and
editorial review determine what is published.

## Source hierarchy

1. Customer, utility, regulator, procurement, or government document
2. DFI, funder, or official implementation-programme document
3. Named implementation partner or transaction document
4. Academic or recognised industry research
5. Reputable reporting with original investigation
6. Provider documentation and case studies
7. Aggregators and commercial databases
8. Community or anonymous submission

Lower-tier sources are useful for discovery but do not independently establish
high-confidence deployment or impact claims.

## Evidence statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `provider_claim_only` | The provider states the claim; no independent evidence has been accepted |
| `public_source` | The claim is supported by a credible public source but independence may be limited |
| `independently_evidenced` | A customer, official, funder, procurement, or genuinely independent source supports it |
| `customer_confirmed` | The named customer or authorised implementation party confirms it |

Provider confirmation of a profile is recorded separately and does not upgrade
deployment evidence.

## Deployment publication test

A verified deployment needs a strong source that identifies the product or
provider, African geography, and customer/project or defensible operating
context. A provider source plus genuine independent corroboration may also pass.

Do not infer deployments from:

- “available in Africa” statements;
- maps without named use cases;
- undated customer-logo pages;
- accelerator participation;
- an office or reseller;
- a provider’s incorporation country; or
- a generic partnership.

## Outcomes

Deployment and outcome are separate assertions. Loss reduction, revenue growth,
uptime, customer count, impact, and savings require their own sources,
measurement periods, and caveats. Provider-reported outcomes are labelled as
such.

## Independence

Independence is determined by origin, not URL count. Syndicated copies of one
press release are one source. A media page reproducing provider copy is not an
independent investigation.

## Market conditions

Category-level findings must include:

- a dated statement;
- its author or editor;
- underlying evidence;
- coverage limitations;
- what would change the finding; and
- a next-review date.

`No verified entry`, `not researched`, and `structurally thin` are distinct.

## Freshness

Published claims display last-checked dates. Stale records remain visible but are
labelled and deprioritised. Historical claims remain available.
