# Data licensing and rights

## Project-created material

Code is MIT licensed. Original documentation and the project-created dataset are
intended for CC BY 4.0 release, subject to the pre-release rights audit described
below.

## Third-party sources

Public availability does not imply permission to reproduce an entire source.
The project generally records facts, metadata, source URLs, locators, and short
evidence notes rather than republishing documents.

Each source should record:

- known licence;
- publisher and attribution;
- access terms;
- whether automated access is permitted;
- whether archival is permitted;
- whether excerpts may be stored;
- whether logos or images may be reused; and
- any geographic or commercial restriction.

## Rights audit

Before a formal data release:

1. review source families and access terms;
2. remove restricted text and media;
3. confirm attribution fields;
4. identify records sourced only from restricted databases;
5. confirm the dataset licence and attribution wording; and
6. document excluded fields and source materials.

## Takedown requests

Rights complaints are assessed separately from factual corrections. Material may
be temporarily hidden while a legitimate rights or safety issue is reviewed,
without deleting the underlying editorial history.
