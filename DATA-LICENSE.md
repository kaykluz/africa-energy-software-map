# Data and documentation licence

Except where stated otherwise, original project documentation and the
project-created dataset are licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International Licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Users must provide appropriate attribution, link to the licence, and indicate
whether changes were made.

## Exclusions

This licence does not relicense:

- third-party reports, webpages, images, logos, or documents;
- quoted or archived source material;
- personal information;
- confidential submissions; or
- content whose rights are held by another party.

Only project-created structure, annotations, classifications, and facts that may
lawfully be shared are included in the open dataset. Source-specific rights and
attribution requirements must be recorded.

The project will complete a source-rights review before the first formal public
data release.

## Organisation marks

Locally stored organisation logos are used only to identify the organisation
that owns the mark. They remain the property of their respective owners, are
excluded from the repository's MIT and CC BY grants, and may be removed on a
substantiated rights request without changing the underlying factual record.
The source page and exact asset URL for each accepted mark are recorded in
`data/brand-assets/organisations.json`. Unapproved marks are not hotlinked and
fall back to a typographic identity tile.
