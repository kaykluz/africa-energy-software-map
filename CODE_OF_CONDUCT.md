# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, evidence-led, and professionally useful
community.

Contributors must:

- engage respectfully across countries, languages, organisations, and roles;
- discuss the evidence and method rather than attack people;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or publication of private
  information;
- accept editorial requests for stronger sourcing; and
- use the correction and appeal process for disputes.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that breach
these expectations. Serious or repeated breaches may result in exclusion from
project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately using the process in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Do not create a public issue containing sensitive
personal information.
